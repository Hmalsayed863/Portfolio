02
Big Data · Federated Learning · Cybersecurity

NeuroGrid — Distributed Agentic Energy Intelligence

A high-performance, fault-tolerant federated learning system built on Apache Spark for smart city energy forecasting. Processes 86.2M rows from ~1,400 households using Spark MLlib KMeans clustering for stratified sampling. Trains 15 independent agents via distributed mapPartitions — each agent learns on private local data. Defends against Byzantine attacks using Multi-Krum filtering (80% acceptance rate). Achieved 91.2% accuracy and 1.7× speedup over gRPC at scale, with an 11% recall improvement for catching energy surges.
